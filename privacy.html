Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: October 8, 2026

DigitalSaudi (“DigitalSaudi,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information through responsible, transparent, and security-conscious practices.

This Privacy Policy explains what information DigitalSaudi may process, why it may be processed, how it may be protected, when it may be shared, and the choices and rights available to you when you use the DigitalSaudi application at digitalsaudi.pi and digitalsaudi.pages.dev, and its services, features, and related experiences (collectively, the “Service”).

DigitalSaudi is designed around data minimization, privacy by design, security, transparency, and user control. We seek to collect and process only information reasonably necessary to provide, secure, improve, and maintain the Service.

By using DigitalSaudi, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where applicable law requires your consent for a particular processing activity, we will request that consent separately.

In short: Most of what you do in DigitalSaudi stays on your device. Our server receives only what a feature needs to work — for example your Pi username when you sign in, payment records for Premium, business listings you submit, and the text you send to the AI assistant. We do not sell data, show third-party ads, use analytics trackers, or ever ask for your Pi Wallet passphrase.

1. Who We Are

DigitalSaudi is an independent digital platform designed to provide information, discovery tools, practical utilities, AI-assisted experiences, travel and daily-life resources, Saudi-related information, and optional digital services. It is available in the Pi Browser at digitalsaudi.pi and on the web at digitalsaudi.pages.dev.

DigitalSaudi is not a government entity, government agency, embassy, immigration authority, bank, financial institution, travel authority, religious authority, or emergency service provider.

DigitalSaudi is also independent of Pi Network.

Pi Network independence

DigitalSaudi is not operated by, owned by, sponsored by, endorsed by, or affiliated with Pi Network, Pi Community Company, the Pi Core Team, or any other Pi-related entity, except where an express written authorization, license, partnership, or official relationship is specifically stated.

Any reference to Pi is intended only to accurately describe supported or compatible functionality where applicable and must not be interpreted as an endorsement, sponsorship, certification, or official relationship.

Pi and related Pi trademarks are owned by their respective rights holders. DigitalSaudi does not claim ownership of them.

2. Information We May Process

The information we process depends on how you use DigitalSaudi and which features you choose to access.

2.1 Information you voluntarily provide

Depending on the feature, this may include:

  • profile preferences;
  • language preferences;
  • accessibility preferences;
  • saved places;
  • saved trips;
  • bookmarks;
  • reminders;
  • favorite content;
  • feedback;
  • support requests;
  • business-directory listings you submit;
  • information you voluntarily submit through forms or other interactions.

Most of these (preferences, saved places, trips, bookmarks, reminders, favorites, CV builder details, emergency card, checklists and chat history) are kept only on your device — see section 2.6. Business listings you submit, together with your Pi username, are stored on our server and published only after review.

We do not require you to provide personal information merely to browse general informational content where the feature can reasonably operate without it. You can use DigitalSaudi as a guest without signing in.

2.2 Pi authentication information

If you choose to use Pi-based authentication, DigitalSaudi may receive the information made available through the applicable Pi authentication mechanism and permitted by the relevant integration. In practice this is your Pi username and Pi user ID, which we use to show your name and link Premium and payments to your account.

We seek to use only the information reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality.

DigitalSaudi does not request or handle wallet secrets

DigitalSaudi does not request, collect, access, transmit, receive, store, display, or ask you to disclose:

  • your Pi Wallet passphrase;
  • seed phrase;
  • private key;
  • wallet secret;
  • account password;
  • authentication password;
  • PIN;
  • verification code or OTP, except where a separate service legitimately provides one through its own secure authentication flow; or
  • other confidential wallet credentials.

Never provide your Pi Wallet passphrase or private key to DigitalSaudi, its personnel, support channels, forms, websites, or any other third party.

Your wallet credentials are private and should remain under your control. Pi Network describes its Pi Wallet as non-custodial and states that the wallet passphrase represents the private key.

If anyone claiming to represent DigitalSaudi asks for your wallet passphrase, private key, seed phrase, password, or similar secret, do not provide it and treat the request as potentially fraudulent.

2.3 Location information

Where you enable location-based functionality, DigitalSaudi may process location information necessary to provide features such as:

  • nearby places;
  • maps;
  • directions;
  • local discovery;
  • weather;
  • location-aware recommendations;
  • travel assistance;
  • emergency-related information.

Location access is requested only when you use a feature that needs it. The Qibla finder uses your location on your device only. “Near me” sends approximate coordinates to our server to look up nearby places from OpenStreetMap; we do not store them. Weather and prayer times use the coordinates of the city you select, not your own position.

You may control location permissions through your device or browser settings. Disabling location access may reduce or prevent certain location-dependent features from functioning.

DigitalSaudi does not use continuous background location tracking.

2.4 Transaction and payment information

DigitalSaudi supports Pi payments for Premium (1 π monthly or 5 π lifetime) and voluntary support payments. Transaction processing may involve information necessary to facilitate, verify, record, reconcile, or display the transaction, including:

  • public wallet addresses;
  • transaction identifiers;
  • payment identifiers;
  • transaction status;
  • transaction amount and plan;
  • timestamps;
  • merchant or recipient information;
  • blockchain-related information.

Payments are approved and completed through the official Pi Network API, and your Premium status is recorded against your Pi user ID. DigitalSaudi does not need and must not request your private wallet credentials to facilitate a legitimate supported transaction.

Accept-Pi posters and pay links are generated on your device. A pay link contains only what you typed (business name, Pi username or wallet address, price, note, city) inside the link itself; we do not store it. Anyone you share the poster or link with can see those details.

Blockchain transactions may be publicly visible and may be permanent or difficult to reverse. Users should carefully verify transaction details before confirming them.

2.5 Technical and diagnostic information

When you use DigitalSaudi, certain technical information may be processed automatically by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) as part of delivering any web page, such as:

  • IP address and approximate region;
  • device type;
  • operating system;
  • browser or application environment;
  • language;
  • network information;
  • application version;
  • security events;
  • performance and diagnostic information.

This information may be used to maintain reliability, troubleshoot problems, prevent abuse, improve performance, and protect the Service. For example, we keep a daily count of AI questions so the free limit can apply — not the questions themselves.

We do not currently use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services, and we seek to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information through technical and diagnostic systems.

2.6 Locally stored information

Many DigitalSaudi features store information locally on your device, including through technologies such as:

  • Local Storage;
  • Session Storage;
  • IndexedDB;
  • service-worker caches;
  • offline storage;
  • application preferences.

Locally stored information may include preferences, language choice, saved content, trips, saved places, bookmarks, favorites, badges, reminders and expiry dates, emergency card details, CV builder details, green-calculator inputs, checklists, AI chat history, payment history, and cached resources for offline use. This information is not uploaded to our server.

The exact information stored locally depends on the features enabled in the version of DigitalSaudi you use.

Clearing application or browser storage — or using Profile › Settings › Clear saved data — removes locally stored preferences, saved content, and offline information.

6. AI Features

DigitalSaudi provides AI-assisted features, including general assistance, travel planning, city information, translation, an Arabic dictionary with pronunciation audio, safety guidance, career assistance, financial-information tools, pilgrimage-related information, and other supported experiences.

How AI requests are handled
  • AI assistant — your question is sent to an AI provider (Google Gemini, Cloudflare Workers AI, or Anthropic Claude) to generate an answer. We store only a daily question count, not your conversation. Chat history stays on your device. Google's free Gemini service may use submitted content to improve its products, so please do not type personal or sensitive information.
  • Translation — when you choose a language other than English, the app's own interface and guide text (never your personal data, messages or inputs) is sent to an AI provider to be translated once, then cached on your device and our server.
  • Arabic dictionary & audio — the word or phrase you look up is sent to Google Gemini to produce the meaning and pronunciation. Results are cached without any link to you.

AI-generated responses may be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, or unsuitable for a particular situation.

Do not provide an AI feature with information you do not wish to disclose unless it is necessary for the requested service.

AI assistance should not be treated as a substitute for:

  • professional legal advice;
  • medical advice;
  • financial advice;
  • official government information;
  • emergency services;
  • qualified religious scholarship.

Where an AI feature uses information from your interaction to provide a response, processing will be subject to the applicable product architecture, settings, and this Privacy Policy.
